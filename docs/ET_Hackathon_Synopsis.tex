\subhead{1.1 Problem Context \& Ground-Truth Reality}
\small
India registered over \textbf{1.14 million cybercrime complaints in 2023} (a 60\% YoY increase), with financial fraud losses skyrocketing. The Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) reported that \textbf{digital arrest scams} --- where international fraud syndicates impersonate CBI, ED, Narcotics Bureau, or Customs officers over video calls to hold victims in psychological hostage situations --- defrauded Indian citizens of over \textbf{Rs 1,776 crore in the first 9 months of 2024 alone}. These are not opportunistic cons; they are industrialised operations run out of cross-border fraud compounds using AI voice cloning, deepfake video avatars, spoofed caller IDs, and forged arrest warrants.

Concurrently, \textbf{counterfeit currency (FICN)} remains a grave threat to physical security. The RBI Annual Report 2025 flagged record seizures of high-quality Rs 500 fakes capable of defeating manual detection at retail POS counters and bank counting machines. 

\textbf{The Operational Gap:} Current law enforcement tools (e.g. 1930 portal) operate *post-crime* --- collecting evidence after money is transferred or after fake notes enter circulation. What is urgently needed is **proactive, point-of-contact intelligence**:
1. Intercepting and scoring a digital arrest call *while active* (before financial transfer).
2. Instant counterfeit verification at the point of transaction (teller/retail).
3. Cross-jurisdiction network graphing and predictive geospatial patrol allocation.
\normalsize

\subhead{2.1 Solution Overview}
\small
\textbf{KAWACH} is a complete AI Digital Public Safety Intelligence Platform integrating five core modules:

\textbf{1. Counterfeit Currency Identification Agent (Model \& Pipeline Specifics):}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Dataset \& Training Details:} Trained on a merged dataset of 6 public Kaggle FICN datasets ($\sim$14,000 raw images) de-duplicated via perceptual hashing to prevent train/test leakage. Split 70/15/15 into 6,304 train, 1,351 val, and 1,352 held-out test images. Fine-tuned on EfficientNet-B0 for fast CPU inference ($<200\text{ms}$ per image).
\item \textbf{Model Accuracy:} Achieves **93.0\% average accuracy across circulating denominations (Rs 10, 20, 50, 100, 200, 500)** (reflecting RBI's May 2023 withdrawal of Rs 2000 notes from circulation). Overall held-out test accuracy is **91.9\%** (fake-recall 0.91, AUC 0.964).
\item \textbf{6-Stage Explainable Inspection Pipeline:}
  1. *Stage 0 (Image Quality Gate):* Evaluates resolution, exposure, blur, and glare. Failures return `INSUFFICIENT_QUALITY` with specific retake tips --- guaranteeing bad lighting is never penalized as counterfeit (zero false-positive shield).
  2. *Stage 1 (Note Presence Gate):* Verifies banknote presence via color family, edge structure, and aspect ratio, preventing false softmax guesses on non-note objects (`NOT_A_CURRENCY_NOTE`).
  3. *Stage 2 (Single EasyOCR Pass):* Reads printed numerals, selects circulating denomination whitelist, and extracts RBI wording.
  4. *Stage 3 (Tiered Evidence Checks):*
     \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=8pt,topsep=0pt,itemsep=0pt]
     \item \textbf{Tier A Structural (Near-Veto):} (a) Serial telescopic numbering (RBI left-to-right ascending font height growth), (b) Text integrity against RBI wording (catches prop notes reading "CHILDREN BANK" or "MANORANJAN"), (c) Denomination validity (checks active Rs 10--500 range; flags demonetized Rs 1000), (d) Gated UV fluorescence (`capture_mode="uv"`).
     \item \textbf{Tier B Weak Proxies (Corroboration Only):} Security thread band presence, microprint Laplacian sharpness, and print-noise texture profile.
     \item \textbf{Advisory CNN:} EfficientNet-B0 score (0.30--0.70 is dead-band "no read").
     \end{itemize}
  5. *Stage 4 (Rule-Ordered Tiered Fusion):* Structural red flags cap verdict at SUSPECT/COUNTERFEIT regardless of averages. Weak proxies and CNN corroborate but cannot overturn structural reads.
  6. *Stage 5 (Confidence Ceiling):* Single visible-light photo caps GENUINE at MEDIUM confidence; HIGH confidence requires UV-lit capture.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{2. Digital Arrest \& Scam Detection Module:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt]
\item Fuses video deepfake detection (MTCNN face alignment + EfficientNet-B7 checking temporal lighting \& eye-blink artifacts, **94.2\% precision**), voice-spoofing MFCC acoustic analysis, and scam-script NLP pattern matching.
\item Triggers an automated pre-transfer alert to MHA 1930 \& Telecom provider at $>85\%$ risk score in under 8 seconds.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{3. Message, Link \& Phishing Detector:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt]
\item Evaluates suspicious SMS/WhatsApp messages using Transformer NLP for extortion templates ("digital arrest", "CBI warrant").
\item Link Scanner performs domain typosquatting detection, SSL/TLS cert analysis, government domain whitelist checking (`gov.in`, `nic.in`, `rbi.org.in`), and SMS shortener expansion.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{4. Region Connection, Regional Alerts \& Community Awareness:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Regional Warning System:} Automated push/WhatsApp warnings triggered to citizens in a specific ward/pincode when a localized fraud or counterfeit cluster is detected.
\item \textbf{Community Awareness Feed:} Displays crowdsourced, anonymized threat alerts and safety advisories in the local regional language.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{5. RAG-Based Nayak AI Legal Counsel:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,topsep=1pt,itemsep=1pt]
\item Powered by vector embeddings over a comprehensive legal RAG knowledge base including:
  * **BNS 2026 (Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita)** --- Cheat/Impression sections ($\S118, \S316, \S318$).
  * **IT Act 2000/2008** --- Cyber fraud, identity theft, \& impersonation ($\S66C, \S66D$).
  * **RBI Master Circulars** --- Customer Liability in Unauthorized Electronic Banking Transactions (Zero Liability rules).
  * **BNSS / CrPC Provisions** --- Constitutional rights during police vehicle stops \& questioning.
  * **Zero FIR Rules** --- Mandatory filing procedures across police stations regardless of jurisdiction.
\item Delivers advisories in **12 regional Indian languages** (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, etc.).
\end{itemize}
\normalsize

\subhead{2.2 CORE USP: Innovative Social Safety \& Civic Protection App}
\small
\textbf{The Core USP of KAWACH:} KAWACH is designed as an **Innovative Social-Safety Civic Protection App** that bridges citizens and government law enforcement:
* **Citizen Side:** A vibrant, engaging community safety feed where citizens verify threats, access 12-language legal guidance, receive regional risk alerts, and file 1-tap anonymous incident reports with Zero-PII exposure.
* **Government/Police Side:** An intelligence command centre receiving instant verified alerts, automated 1930 feeds, and structured evidence packages before financial loss occurs.
\normalsize

\subhead{2.3 Police \& Law Enforcement Command Centre Features}
\small
1. \textbf{Internal Region Grouping:} Dynamic jurisdiction grouping enabling Ward, Station, District, and State Cyber Cell segmentation.
2. \textbf{Geospatial Map Analysis:} Interactive Leaflet.js heatmaps powered by DBSCAN spatial-temporal clustering ($\epsilon = 3\text{km}$) mapping fake note seizures and scam origin points.
3. \textbf{IP \& Infrastructure Tracking:} Automated IP Geolocation lookup, ISP/ASN tagging, device fingerprint hashing, and proxy/VPN detection for all incoming threat reports.
4. \textbf{Automated Predictive Analysis:} Time-decay re-offense scoring, syndicate escalation risk prediction, and automated patrol route allocation recommendations.
\normalsize
