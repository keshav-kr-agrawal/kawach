S·02

The stakes are counted in savings.

A "digital arrest" call keeps a victim on video for hours, impersonating the CBI, until a lifetime's savings move in one transfer. A pothole stays unreported because nobody knows which of eleven departments owns it. Both failures share one root: no single system watches the report from evidence to action.

  • ₹120+ crore lost to digital-arrest scams in Karnataka alone (2024).
  • Hours, not minutes — the average scam call runs long enough to intercept.
  • Zero convictions follow most civic complaints that die in routing.
S·03

What KAWACH is.

A public-safety grid in three surfaces: a citizen app that captures evidence and talks to a legal counsel, a department grid that answers on a clock, and a police console that reads the city's patterns. One classifier microservice sits under all three, and identity never crosses the citizen boundary.

    How a report travels.

    Capture

    Citizen films the hazard or uploads the suspect note in chat. EXIF scrubbed on device.

    Verify

    The classifier runs deepfake, scene, and currency forensics on the media — before anything is filed.

    Confirm

    Detection never files. The citizen sees the verdict and explicitly confirms the report.

    Route

    Zero-shot department mapping + urgency scoring drops it into the right queue with an SLA running.

    Act

    Departments resolve on the clock; police see the pattern; breaches surface on the master console.

    S·07

    Pipeline 1 — Deepfake forensics.

    Every video is sampled 32 frames deep; MTCNN finds the faces and a dual EfficientNet-B7 ensemble votes on whether they're real. Synthetic evidence dies before it becomes a complaint.

    • MTCNN + 2× EfficientNet-B7, frame-consensus verdict with confidence and face count.
    S·08

    Pipeline 2 — Routing & urgency.

    Free text becomes a department and a priority: zero-shot mapping picks the desk, a DistilBERT urgency model cross-checks the tier, and a keyword fallback keeps routing alive even with no network.

    • Gemini routing + DistilBERT priority, honest keyword fallback when offline.
    S·09

    Pipeline 3 — Scene verification.

    Does the video actually show what the report claims? YOLO12s reads road damage, a SigLIP classifier reads waste scenes, and a temporal-consistency gate rejects one-frame illusions.

    • YOLO12s + SigLIP with a ≥0.5 persistence ratio across sampled frames.
    S·10

    Pipeline 4 — Trust fusion.

    Deepfake confidence, scene consistency, and text urgency fuse into two deterministic 0–100 scores: how much to trust the media, and how fast to dispatch. Same inputs, same score, every time.

    • Unified Trust Score + Civic Urgency Score — arithmetic, not vibes.
    S·11

    Pipeline 5 — Hotspot clustering.

    Incidents cluster on real haversine distance with DBSCAN. Clusters become patrol targets with centroid, dominant crime type, and max threat; isolated points stay honestly labeled as noise.

    • DBSCAN (haversine), tunable radius and density from the console.
    S·12

    Pipeline 6 — Counterfeit currency.

    A CNN trained on 6,304 merged, de-duplicated note images — plus four classical checks: security-thread continuity, microprint sharpness, print-noise profile, and RBI's own telescopic serial-number pattern read by OCR. Disagreement means INCONCLUSIVE, disclosed.

    • 98.67% held-out accuracy, AUC 0.998; UV checks honestly gated to UV-lit captures.
    S·13

    Pipeline 7 — Digital-arrest interception.

    A live scam call becomes a monitored session. Script phrases, voice-spoof probability, deepfake video, and transaction anomalies fuse into one score — and at seventy, dispatch fires before the transfer completes.

    • Weighted fusion ·30/·20/·20/·30 + corroboration bonus, audit-logged pre-transfer alert.
    S·14

    S·17

    Follow the money's shape.

    Offenders, phones, accounts, UPI handles and devices form one graph. Louvain community detection surfaces the rings; betweenness centrality names the brokers; and clean-history nodes wired into high-risk rings get an explainable mule flag — with the reasoning spelled out in words.

    • Louvain + betweenness + mule heuristics, every flag carries its rationale.
    S·18

    Evidence that holds up.

    Dossiers generate from live database rows into real PDFs, hashed SHA-256 over the final bytes, recorded in an immutable audit log, with the chain-of-custody statement printed in the footer. The hash on screen must match the file in court.

    • BSA §63 admissibility intent, hash-sealed, audit-logged exports.
    S·19

    Check before the money moves.

    A phone number, UPI handle, or link — checked against criminal records plus two behavioral signals: a dormant line bursting with calls, and the receive-and-forward mule shape. High-risk hits come back with a pre-drafted NCRP complaint.

    • Records + behavior fusion, rationale disclosed on every verdict.
    S·20

    Honest when degraded.

    When a live service is unreachable, KAWACH says so — a red dot in the corner — and falls back deterministically: keyword routing, statistical scoring, simulated ledgers that are behaviorally identical and honestly flagged. Nothing ever fabricates a verdict and calls it real.
