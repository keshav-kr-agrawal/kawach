citizen Vercel — React/Vite PWA

Snapchat-style platform: map, video, reels, AI chat, services

  • ▸social feed / reelsUSP
    Every video or photo posted — whether shot for the Reels feed or attached to a report — passes through the AI detection layer. If it's identified as a live local incident (a pothole, a street fight, a visible counterfeit note), it auto-surfaces as a reel to other users in that same area. This happens whether or not anyone files a formal complaint — it's crowdsourced situational awareness, and it's why people open the app like a social feed, not just during emergencies. Cloudinary — reel storage Leaflet + OSM — geo-match to nearby users
  • ▸AI detection layer — Nayak assistant
    Always-on scanner. It never files a complaint by itself — it classifies what's in the upload, scores severity, and works out which department it belongs to, then asks the citizen to confirm before anything becomes an official complaint. Gemini 2.5 / 3.x Flash free tier
    • ▸text detection
      Scam-script and phishing-language patterns in chat, SMS, or forwarded text.
    • ▸video detection
      Deepfake face-forensics, plus general scene classification — is this a pothole, a fire, a fight, roadkill, a crowd. HF Spaces — MTCNN + EfficientNet-B7, YOLO12s scene model
    • ▸voice detection
      AI-cloned or spoofed voice on a call, and raw loud-noise/disturbance detection from ambient audio. HF Spaces — voice-spoof + audio-event classifier
    • ▸image detection (currency)
      Real/fake verdict on a photographed note from microprint and security-thread analysis.
    • ▸link detection
      Checks shared links/QR codes against known phishing and fraudster site patterns.
    • ▸AI classifier (fusion)
      Combines every signal above into one output: issue type, severity, and the specific department it should route to.
    • ▸confirm & routebridge
      The one moment detection and reporting connect. Citizen sees "we think this is a road hazard — raise it as a complaint?" and taps yes or no. A "no" still lets the clip surface in Reels; only a "yes" creates a formal complaint in a department queue.
  • ▸reportingseparate path
    Independent of the feed and independent of AI detection — the citizen picks a category directly and submits. The classifier still runs on any attached evidence (e.g. verifying a currency photo), but it doesn't gate whether the report gets filed. Supabase — Postgres + Realtime
    • ▸crimes
      Routes to the police crime classifier.
    • ▸emergencies
      Dual-routes to whichever of fire / police / health fits, flagged at the top SLA tier.
    • ▸gov department issues
      Manual version of the same routing the AI classifier does automatically — for citizens who'd rather just pick the department themselves.
    • ▸text reporting
      Free-text complaints for anything that doesn't fit a structured category.
    • ▸counterfeit
      Backed by the image detection (currency) classifier above.
    • ▸digital arrest / active scam session
      Fuses voice + video + text detection into one live risk score before financial transfer happens — the platform's headline flow.
  • ▸anonymity guarantee
    Every report — from Reels-detected or direct — carries only an anonymous tracking ID. No name, phone number, or account identity is ever attached or visible to any department dashboard. This is the platform's core trust promise: report anything, reveal nothing.
  • ▸Citizen Fraud Shield — WhatsApp
    Same fraud-risk assessment as the app, reachable from WhatsApp for citizens who won't install a new app mid-scam call. WhatsApp Cloud API free tier
department layer Render free — FastAPI router · Supabase Realtime

Where every confirmed AI-detected issue and every direct report lands, auto-sorted to the right desk

Every department dashboard is identical in structure — a live severity-sorted alert queue, a map of active issues in that department's jurisdiction, a status workflow (new → acknowledged → in progress → resolved), and its own analytics panel. Only the category of what lands there differs.
▸SLA & escalation engineshared infra
Severity sets a timer — critical 15 min, high 4 hrs, medium 24 hrs, low 72 hrs. If a department hasn't acted before the timer lapses, the issue turns red on that department's own dashboard and pushes into the Master Admin Dashboard's escalation feed for oversight. Reports that touch more than one department — a pothole causing a traffic hazard, for instance — dual-route to both queues automatically.

▸police crime classifier
The deepest branch — street fights, crime reports, and digital-arrest sessions land here. Same dashboard structure as every other department, plus the graph-intelligence layer below. Neo4j Aura free tier, JSON fallback
  • ▸AI analytics
    Case-level intelligence built from every routed report.
    • ▸relation mapping
      Graph linking suspects to gangs, vehicles, phones, UPI accounts, CDR pings. NetworkX
    • ▸criminal mapping (neural network)
      Learned embeddings surfacing non-obvious connections between cases.
    • ▸repeat offender tracking
      Flags individuals reappearing across separately-filed reports.
    • ▸predictive risk scoring
      Scores a case or area by escalation likelihood.
    • ▸crime hotspot + trend/anomaly alerts
      Flags statistically unusual spikes before they're obvious from raw volume.
    • ▸geospatial fraud + counterfeit hotspot layer

      District-level policy insight from crime density vs. socio-economic indicators.

    Single-suspect dossier — history, records, analysis, mapping, and predictive re-offense score.

    Receives the fused risk score in real time — can alert dispatch before a victim transfers money.
  • ▸auditable evidence export

▸master admin dashboard
One view across every department — total issues, resolution rate per department, a city-wide heatmap, a department performance leaderboard, and a live escalation feed for anything that breached its SLA. This is the single screen that shows the whole government side of the platform at a glance. Supabase aggregate views + Chart.js/Recharts
